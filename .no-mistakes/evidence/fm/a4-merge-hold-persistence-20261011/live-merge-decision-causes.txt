Durable merge-decision reader refusal causes (real bin/fm-merge-decision-lib.sh)
valid identity + head          -> ACCEPT decision=PASS
mismatched head SHA            -> REFUSE reason=head-mismatch
mismatched PR number           -> REFUSE reason=identity-mismatch
lapsed (at expiry)             -> REFUSE reason=expired
current (1s before expiry)     -> ACCEPT decision=PASS
absent record                  -> REFUSE reason=absent
malformed (extra field)        -> REFUSE reason=malformed
unreadable (mode 0644)         -> REFUSE reason=unreadable
recorded HOLD read back        -> ACCEPT decision=HOLD
